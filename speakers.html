Expert sessions at SSC ’26

NK

Nagendra K

Founder & Director, Research Heights Foundation

  • Co-Chair, ESG & Sustainability Committee, FTCCI
  • Angel Investor
  • Strategic Institutional Advisor

09:30 AM – 10:15 AM, Main Hall

Engineering Sustainable Futures: From Campus Innovation to Community Impact

Founder & Director of Research Heights Foundation, a not-for-profit organisation working at the intersection of research, innovation, sustainability and institutional transformation. Through the Bharat Environment Program 2027 – National Sustainable Campus Mission, he is advancing a Campus-Industry-Community model that connects student and faculty capacity building with climate action, innovation challenges, community engagement and evidence-based impact.

“Do not prepare only to be job-ready; prepare to be problem-ready, collaboration-ready and impact-ready.”

Senior Vice President, DBS Tech India

Industry Relations Chair, IEEE Hyderabad Section

Token Economics in the Age of AI

A technology leader with over 20 years of experience in innovation, solution design and strategic development.

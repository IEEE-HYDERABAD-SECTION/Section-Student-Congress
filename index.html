About SSC ’26

IEEE Hyderabad Section Student Activities Committee presents Section Student Congress ’26, bringing together IEEE Student Members, Student Branch leaders, volunteers and innovators from across the Hyderabad Section.

Under the theme “Engineering a Sustainable Society Through Collaboration,” SSC ’26 creates a platform for students to exchange ideas and best practices, strengthen Student Branch communities, engage with IEEE leaders and professionals, and collaborate on solutions for a more sustainable future.

From expert sessions and student innovation showcases to IEEE Day activities, networking and collaborative challenges, the congress is designed around one idea: Connect. Collaborate. Innovate. Sustain.

IEEE Day × SSC ’26

SSC ’26 will also host the Hyderabad Section’s student celebration of IEEE Day.

Participants will come together across institutions through eight sustainability-focused colour teams, encouraging students from different colleges to connect and collaborate throughout the day.

The programme includes a collaborative Problem Wall, interactive IEEE and sustainability challenges, team activities and short problem pitches linked to challenges observed on campuses and in Hyderabad.

The winning colour team will be recognised during the Valedictory Session.

Student Branch Poster Competition

Tell the story of your Student Branch’s impact.

The SSC ’26 Student Branch Poster Competition challenges branches to go beyond listing activities and instead showcase a problem they addressed, what they did, measurable outcomes and where collaboration with other Student Branches can take the work next.

Project Expo

Innovations for a Sustainable Society

Student teams will showcase working prototypes, early prototypes, software solutions and simulations addressing real challenges across sustainability-focused areas.

Projects are encouraged to communicate not only what works, but also their sustainability considerations, present limitations and opportunities for collaboration.

Interactive Leadership Panel

Not a sequence of prepared speeches.

The SSC ’26 panel is designed around student questions and real challenges raised during the congress.

Questions will emerge from the Problem Wall, participant submissions and live interaction with the audience, creating a direct conversation between students and IEEE Hyderabad Section leadership.

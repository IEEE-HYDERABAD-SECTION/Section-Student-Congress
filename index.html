About SSC ’26

IEEE Hyderabad Section Student Activities Committee presents Section Student Congress ’26, bringing together IEEE Student Members, Student Branch leaders, volunteers and innovators from across the Hyderabad Section.

Under the theme “Engineering a Sustainable Society Through Collaboration,” SSC ’26 creates a platform for students to exchange ideas and best practices, strengthen Student Branch communities, engage with IEEE leaders and professionals, and collaborate on solutions for a more sustainable future.

From expert sessions and student innovation showcases to IEEE Day activities, networking and collaborative challenges, the congress is designed around one idea: Connect. Collaborate. Innovate. Sustain.

IEEE Day × SSC ’26

SSC ’26 will also host the Hyderabad Section’s student celebration of IEEE Day.

Participants will come together across institutions through eight sustainability-focused colour teams, encouraging students from different colleges to connect and collaborate throughout the day.

The programme includes a collaborative Problem Wall, interactive IEEE and sustainability challenges, team activities and short problem pitches linked to challenges observed on campuses and in Hyderabad.

The winning colour team will be recognised during the Valedictory Session.

The eight colour teams

Every student receives a coloured wristband at registration. The colour is your team for the day, and each team stands for one sustainability focus area linked to a UN Sustainable Development Goal.

  • Team Energy (Yellow) Clean and affordable energy, SDG 7
  • Team Water (Blue) Clean water and sanitation, SDG 6
  • Team Climate (Green) Climate action, SDG 13
  • Team Circular (Orange) Responsible consumption and e-waste, SDG 12
  • Team Health (Pink) Good health and well-being, SDG 3
  • Team Learn (Purple) Quality education, SDG 4
  • Team Build (Red) Industry, innovation and infrastructure, SDG 9
  • Team City (White) Sustainable cities and communities, SDG 11

The Problem Wall

The wall is open all day from 8:30 AM. Pair up with someone from your colour team who is from a different college, and write one problem you have seen in Hyderabad or on your campus that fits your team’s focus area. Notes on the wall by 1:00 PM can be picked for the afternoon pitch round.

Student Branch Poster Competition

Tell the story of your Student Branch’s impact.

The SSC ’26 Student Branch Poster Competition challenges branches to go beyond listing activities and instead showcase a problem they addressed, what they did, measurable outcomes and where collaboration with other Student Branches can take the work next.

Enter the Poster Competition

Entries close on 7 October 2026 at 11:59 PM. Everyone taking part must also register for SSC ’26.

Entry format, eligibility and awards

Eligibility

  • One poster per Student Branch.
  • At least 2 branch members registered for SSC ’26 and present at the poster.

Poster format

  • A1 portrait (594 × 841 mm), printed. Mounting boards and pins or tape are provided.
  • Four boxes: the problem we tackled, what we did, measurable results, what we want to do next.
  • A “Looking for Partners” line with a contact.
  • About 250 words in total. At least one box must connect to sustainability or collaboration.

Steal This Event cards

Bring 50 printed cards explaining how to run your best event. Visitors take a card for any event they want to run at their own college.

Awards

Best SB Poster, Runner-up SB Poster, Most Stolen Event and People’s Choice.

Project Expo

Innovations for a Sustainable Society

Student teams will showcase working prototypes, early prototypes, software solutions and simulations addressing real challenges across sustainability-focused areas.

Projects are encouraged to communicate not only what works, but also their sustainability considerations, present limitations and opportunities for collaboration.

Enter the Project Expo

Entries close on 7 October 2026 at 11:59 PM. Everyone taking part must also register for SSC ’26.

Eligibility, what to bring, safety and awards

Eligibility

  • Teams of 2 to 4 students, all registered for SSC ’26.
  • A working prototype, an early prototype, or a software or simulation demo. Concept-only entries are not accepted.
  • Each project is entered under one of the eight sustainability focus areas listed above.

What to bring

  • Your project and laptop, with chargers.
  • A Sustainability Label on A4: power use, build cost, materials, expected life and end of life.
  • A “What Doesn’t Work Yet” board on A4: current limitations and the help you need.
  • An A1 poster about the project (optional).

Safety

  • No open flames, heating elements, or hazardous chemicals or liquids.
  • No flying drones or projectiles indoors.
  • Batteries in good condition, and all cables taped down.

Awards

Best Project, Runner-up Project, People’s Choice, Best Sustainability Label and up to 4 Speaker Picks.
